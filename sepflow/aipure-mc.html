AIpure-MC

AIpure-MC is a flexible, intuitive, fast and reliable dual-pump chromatography system with a flow rate range of 0.001–25 mL/min, with 100 mL and 150 mL versions available as options. It comes standard with dual-wavelength UV (190–400 nm), conductivity, temperature and pH monitoring, ultrasonic bubble sensors, three titanium pressure sensors and an open fraction collector. Inlet, column and outlet valves, a sample pump and a sample inlet valve can be added to match each workflow.

Key features
  • Dual piston-pump system with a flow rate range of 0.001–25 mL/min (100 mL and 150 mL versions optional)
  • Linear, isocratic and step gradients
  • A/B pumps come standard with 2 inlets each (4 in total), upgradeable to an 8-channel inlet valve on each pump; pure PEEK construction
  • Standard single-position column valve supporting bypass, down-flow and up-flow modes; optional 5-position valve; PEEK, pressure resistant to 20 MPa
  • Standard 2 outlets, optional 12-outlet large-volume collection valve; pure PEEK
  • Injection valve resistant to 20 MPa, supporting sample loading, loading and feeding modes
  • Dual-wavelength UV detector, 190–400 nm, with both wavelengths adjustable simultaneously and a default 2 mm optical path
  • Conductivity detection from 0–999.999 mS/cm and pH detection from 0–14, both with automatic temperature compensation; temperature reading 0–100 °C
  • Ultrasonic bubble sensor after each A and B pump to monitor sample and system bubble formation
  • Three standard titanium pressure sensors: system, pre-column and post-column pressure
  • Open fraction collector for 180 × 1.5 mL, 120 × 15 mL or 42 × 50 mL centrifuge tubes
  • Optional pH valve with bypass, online and calibration modes
  • Optional sample pump (25, 100 or 150 mL/min), sample pump pressure sensor and 2- or 8-channel sample inlet valve
